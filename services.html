What Kintrist does

Every package draws on the same set of services.

Appointment advocacy

We schedule doctor appointments in every package. In the Coordination and Full Advocacy packages, Hillary also meets your loved one at the appointment and helps make sure questions get asked, answers are understood and next steps are followed up.

In-person check-ins

Regular visits with your loved one at home, so someone who knows them is seeing how they're really doing.

Shared appointment calendar

One shared calendar that your family can see, kept up to date, including everyday appointments such as grooming or dental appointments.

Document organization

Important papers gathered, sorted and kept current, so they can be found when they're needed.

Medication list management

We keep an up-to-date list of your loved one's medications to bring to appointments, and we help keep refills on track with the pharmacy and doctor's office. We don't give medications or make decisions about them.

Connecting to resources

We know the Tri-Cities. When your loved one needs a service or a provider, we help your family find the right one.

Fraud monitoring

With view-only access, we watch bank accounts and let your family know about unusual activity. We never pay bills or move money.

Family updates

Regular updates on how your loved one is doing and what's coming up. How often depends on your package, from monthly to weekly.

What Kintrist doesn't do

  • No hands-on or medical care.
  • We do not drive your loved one to appointments. Hillary meets them there.
  • We do not pay bills or handle money.
  • We do not sign on your loved one's behalf.

If your loved one needs hands-on help, we can help your family find a provider.

Where we work

Kintrist serves Kennewick, Pasco, Richland and communities within about 30 to 40 miles of the Tri-Cities.

Your family can be anywhere. Many of the people we work for live in another city or state.
