Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Kintrist ("we," "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you email us or use the contact form, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, email address, phone number, your parent's city and your message.
  • Basic technical information. Like most websites, our hosting provider automatically records limited technical data, such as IP address, browser type and the pages visited, to keep the site secure and working.

Please do not send medical details through this website.

How we use information

  • To respond to your questions and schedule calls
  • To provide and improve our services and this website
  • To keep the website secure
  • To meet legal obligations

How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host and email provider, when the law requires it, or when you ask us to.

Client and health information

If you become a client, any health or personal information we handle for you or your parent is collected and shared only with written consent, under separate client agreements and consent forms. Those documents, not this website policy, describe how that information is used.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy.

How long we keep information

We keep messages and contact details only as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Email hillary@kintrist.com and we will respond.

Children

This website is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email hillary@kintrist.com.
