Meet Hillary.

Hillary Legard

Founder and Family Advocate

Hillary spent nearly 20 years as a radiologic technologist and then moved into healthcare administration. She knows how clinics and hospitals work from the inside: how appointments get scheduled, where information gets lost and which questions to ask.

She has also lived the other side of it. Hillary spent much of her adult life looking after her own parents and coordinating their care.

After a first call, I want every family to feel safe, and to know that there's help.

Kintrist brings those two things together: a professional who understands healthcare, and a person who understands what your family is carrying.
