When you can't be there, we are.

Many families are managing a loved one's care from across town or across the country. Kintrist gives you one trusted person who knows your loved one, keeps a shared calendar of what's coming up and tells you what's happening.

At the appointment

We schedule your loved one's appointments, meet them there, ask the questions that need asking and make sure nothing gets lost afterward.

Keeping things organized

A shared calendar, paperwork and important documents are kept in order, so the right information is on hand when it's needed.

Keeping you informed

Regular in-person check-ins with your loved one and regular updates for you. No more piecing the story together from phone calls.

What we do, and what we don't.

Kintrist is an advocate and coordinator. We work alongside your loved one's doctors and any care providers; we don't replace them.

We do

  • Schedule doctor appointments and meet your loved one there
  • Speak up for your loved one and your family
  • Visit in person to check in
  • Organize documents and keep a shared appointment calendar
  • Connect you with local resources
  • Watch bank accounts, view-only, and alert you to unusual activity

We don't

  • Provide hands-on or medical care
  • Drive your loved one to appointments
  • Pay bills or move money
  • Sign anything on your loved one's behalf

How it starts

  1. A first call

    We listen. You tell us about your loved one, your family and what you're worried about.

  2. A getting-started visit

    Hillary meets your loved one at home, talks with the family and puts together a plan and a list of helpful resources.

  3. Steady, ongoing support

    Check-ins, appointments and paperwork are handled month to month, with regular updates to you.

Start with a conversation.
